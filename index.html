Already Applied? What's Next?

After submitting your college application, remember to:

  • Check your college application portal and email regularly.
  • Submit requested transcripts or additional documents.
  • Complete your FAFSA and add your college school codes.
  • Review admission decisions, financial aid offers, and enrollment steps.
